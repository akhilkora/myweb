About

I'm Akhil Kora, a tenth-grade student at Adlai E. Stevenson High School in Buffalo Grove, IL, on a competitive robotics team with hands-on experience in CAD, Arduino, and programming.

On FTC Team #24108 "Boltic" I’m the Design Lead on the Design & Hardware subteams. I designed a custom coaxial swerve drivetrain in Fusion 360 to improve steering and holonomic motion on the field, then built the parallel-plate chassis and turret myself — picking up CNC manufacturing, 3D printing, and soldering along the way.

Outside the workshop, I’m co-founding a non-profit that teaches middle schoolers the fundamentals of patent law and intellectual property through hands-on workshops.

I’m looking for a summer internship or research opportunity to build practical engineering skills and contribute reliably to a team.
